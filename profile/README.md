# Coherence Energy Labs

**How systems hold together.**

Coherence Energy Labs is a software and applied-systems company built on **coherence energy** — one measurable way to know how well any system holds together, and to make it hold together better.

The things we study in separate rooms — physics, life, mind, and technology — are more deeply connected than our disciplines treat them. Our work asks whether that connection can be turned into something measurable, testable, and engineerable.

## A claim is only as strong as the artifact behind it

Everything we ship carries its evidence with it: programs that produce cryptographic receipts, results a stranger can re-execute and verify independently, and gates that can say no. We build systems that do not hide how they work — that can be inspected, that can prove what happened, and that preserve coherence instead of destroying it.

## What we build

- **Coherence Language** — a first-principles programming language with certified compilation, where the same program runs bit-identically across backends
- **A.C.E** — coherent intelligence: an AI architecture whose reasoning is auditable end to end
- **obsign** — AI you can prove
- **Live demos** — re-executable figures and interactive systems where every chart is a program, its input, and its receipt: [demos.coherenceenergylabs.com](https://demos.coherenceenergylabs.com)
