The law

Coherence energy: what it costs to hold a system together against noise

Our thesis is a single quantity and a single equation. The quantity is the thermodynamic cost of a system's order:

E_coh = k_B·T · D_KL( ρ_system ‖ ρ_disorder )

And that order is distributed over a system's structure as a field, governed by a screened Helmholtz equation solved on the system's own graph:

(D·L + κ²I) τ = s

Where L is the Laplacian of whatever graph the system actually is — gates and wires, functions and calls, nodes and links — and s is where disorder enters. Solve it, and τ tells you where the system is straining to stay coherent.

The claim is that this is substrate-independent. Change the graph, change nothing else, and it should still predict. That is a falsifiable claim, and the rest of this document is us trying to kill it.

Why this cannot be an ML surrogate

Every learned power model is trained on simulation output and inherits its distribution. Ours was never trained on anything — κ is a physical constant we froze — and it got better when the workload changed. That is the signature of a law rather than a fit, and it is the one property the incumbent approach cannot buy with more data.

The principle

And it yields a design rule nobody has written down

The correlation is negative, and the reason is the discovery.

The design goal is not less hazard.
It is more localized hazard.

Two netlists, identical gate count, provably identical function. A diffuse field carries glitches across the whole circuit; a localized one absorbs them. Every low-power methodology in use today tries to reduce total hazard. The field says concentrate it instead — and the measurement agrees, at ρ = −0.817 out of sample.

The identity s + 2c == a + b + c is proved over all inputs, along with every resolver and every carry-save reduction order — so the two circuits are not similar, they are provably the same function.

Why no synthesis flow on earth finds this:

  • It lives in the number system, not the Boolean netlist. It is invisible from inside a standard-cell mapper, which starts after the representation is already chosen.
  • It spends gates to save energy — which an area-minimising flow refuses by construction.

Every commercial tool optimises area and timing and treats power as a report. This is a whole category of savings sitting above the layer they operate on, and it is reachable only if you are reasoning about the physics rather than the gates.

How you know it's real

We published that number, then attacked it, and published what we found

At shallow depth, on a real nonce sweep, carry-save is worse. The advantage we had called structural was partly manufactured by our own random stimulus. In the campaign's own words: "The convergence phase 54 reported is real; the inference was not."

The advantage survives — it is real at mining depth, and smaller than published at every depth measured. And the re-measurement handed us a free lever: gray-coded nonce ordering recovers about half the loss at no implementation cost.

The transfer

Same operator. Different substrate. It works on software.

If coherence energy is a real property of complex systems rather than a circuit trick, the identical equation should predict where programs spend energy — no retraining, no new theory, just a different graph.

It is already in the compiler. tau_field.py — 745 lines, eight consumers including the bug hunter, the fabric partitioner and the scheduler. Its own docstring:

coherence_lang/compiler/tau_field.py
Solves the screened Helmholtz equation on the CIR call graph to
compute the tau_c value of every function / block / instruction.

    (D * L + kappa^2 * I) * tau_c = source

The tau_c field replaces profiling. The compiler knows which code
is hot from the structure of the computation graph -- before
execution.

Gates and wires. Functions and calls. The same operator, with the same physical meaning, running in production in two substrates that share nothing else.

And the honest state of it: on circuits this is measured, with controls, a permutation null and a frozen held-out parameter. On programs it is built and consumed but not yet measured — no benchmark campaign has scored τ_c against real profiler output. That experiment is the first thing this round funds, because it is the one that decides whether we have a tool or a law.

Why this is defensible

Not the software. Anyone can implement a Helmholtz solve on a graph in a week. The moat is three things a competitor cannot clone from the outside:

  • Knowing what to put in s. Arrival skew was the fourth thing we tried. The kill ledger has seventy-one entries because sixty-odd source terms and observables did not work. That negative space is the asset, and it is not in any paper.
  • Proof-carrying equivalence. Ranking designs is meaningless unless the variants are provably the same function. We prove it over all inputs, which is why a 25% claim from us is checkable rather than plausible.
  • A methodology that spends gates to save energy. Structurally opposed to how every incumbent flow is built. Adopting it is not a feature they can ship — it inverts their objective function.
